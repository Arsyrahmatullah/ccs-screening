METHODOLOGY / 01

Storage Readiness
Level (SRL).

Following Nooraiepour et al. (2025), we classify basins on a 1-9 maturity scale. Our prototype focuses on the critical jump from SRL 1 (Inventory) to SRL 2 (Systematically Mapped).

METHODOLOGY / 02

Tectonic
Integrity Filters.

Geology is the first gate. We prioritize Back-Arc & Passive Margin settings for their superior reservoir-seal duos, while active Fore-Arc basins are disqualified for structural risk.

METHODOLOGY / 03

Triple Cut-off
Verification.

For site-specific screening, we apply three simultaneous gates: Porosity (%), CO2 Density (kg/m³), and Fault Setbacks (km) to identify high-integrity clusters.

01 / EMISSION GRAVITY

Every storage story starts above ground.

Great-circle distance and a gravity-style accessibility index bring emission clusters and prospective basins into one transparent screen.

02 / RESERVOIR FIELD

Then we travel below the surface.

Porosity, CO2 density, and fault distance create a first-pass picture of which parts of a formation can safely enter the conversation.

03 / CONNECTIVITY

Potential becomes a place when it holds together.

DBSCAN separates isolated points from connected optimal zones, retaining only areas that satisfy a project-scale threshold.

04 / CAPACITY RANGE

The honest answer is never one number.

Monte Carlo sampling reports P10, P50, and P90 outcomes so uncertainty stays part of the result, not a footnote.
